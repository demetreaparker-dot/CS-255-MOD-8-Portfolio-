# CS-255-MOD-8-Portfolio-
Course Projects
Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The DriverPass project involved designing a system for DriverPass, a company that helps students prepare for DMV driving exams. The client wanted a cloud-based web application that would allow customers to register for services, access online practice tests and training materials, schedule driving lessons, and track their progress. The system also needed to support employees by allowing them to manage customers, appointments, instructors, vehicles, and training packages.

What did you do particularly well?

I believe I did particularly well translating the client’s requirements into a structured system design. I developed business requirements and UML diagrams that reflected the needs discussed during the interview process. I also focused on making the system secure, organized, and easy for multiple user groups to access and manage.

If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve the UML diagrams by adding more detailed interactions and clearer process flow between users and system components. I would also include additional examples of exception handling and system responses to provide a more complete design.

How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?

I interpreted the user’s needs by reviewing the DriverPass interview transcript and identifying the goals and challenges discussed by the client. I implemented those needs by designing features such as online scheduling, account management, practice testing, reporting, and secure access controls. Considering user needs is important because a successful system must solve the client’s problem while remaining easy and efficient for users to interact with.

How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

I approach designing software by first understanding the client’s objectives and gathering requirements before creating technical solutions. I organize requirements into functional and nonfunctional categories and use diagrams to visualize the system structure and processes. In future projects, I would continue using requirement analysis, UML modeling, stakeholder feedback, and iterative design improvements to create systems that are scalable, secure, and user focused.
